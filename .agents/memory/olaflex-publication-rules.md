## Rule

Do not invent predictions, results, testimonials, or win records. Keep public pages empty until real records are published by the administrator. Never expose premium booking codes, selections, match details, analysis, images, or odds publicly unless a verified payment/access check explicitly permits them.

**Why:** The user requires accurate records and says premium details must stay protected until access is verified.

**How to apply:** Enforce these rules in database seeding/import behavior, public API responses, and public UI. Keep administrator access server-verified and restricted to the configured account without copying its email into project memory.
